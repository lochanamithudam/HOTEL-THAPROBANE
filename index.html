A Regal Legacy

Inspired by the Ancient Isle of Pearls

Perched majestically upon the palm-fringed cliffs of Sri Lanka’s south-western shoreline, Hotel Thaprobane is an ode to classical Ceylon sophistication. Named after the ancient Greek moniker for this paradise isle, our sanctuary blends tropical modernist architecture by Geoffrey Bawa inspirations with bespoke 5-star oceanfront grandeur.

Wake to the gentle melody of breaking turquoise waves, inhale the scent of wild cinnamon blossoms, and let our dedicated private butlers curate an unforgettable Sri Lankan retreat.
