Guest Guide & Information

Hotel Policies & Sanctuary Guidelines

To preserve an atmosphere of serene luxury, restorative tranquility, and uncompromising privacy for every guest, we kindly invite you to review our check-in, cancellation, and villa guidelines below.

Check-in & Check-out

  • Check-in Time: From 14:00 (2:00 PM)
  • Check-out Time: Until 12:00 (12:00 PM)
  • Early Check-in: Subject to availability; complimentary luggage holding & lounge access provided.
  • Late Check-out: Complimentary up to 14:00 upon request (based on villa availability).
  • Express In-Villa Check-in: Welcome king coconut & registration conducted in your villa.

Cancellation & Modifications

  • Free Cancellation: Up to 48 hours prior to scheduled arrival date for standard direct reservations.
  • Late Cancellation / No-Show: Cancellations within 48 hours will incur a fee equivalent to 1 night's stay.
  • Zero Prepayment Required: Direct bookings require no upfront deposit; pay comfortably upon check-in.
  • Date Modification: One complimentary date adjustment permitted up to 24 hours prior to arrival.

Children & Extra Beds

  • Children Welcome: Guests of all ages are warmly welcomed across all private plunge villas and suites.
  • Ages 0 to 5: Stay complimentary when utilizing existing bedding. Baby cribs / cots are provided free of charge.
  • Ages 6 to 12: Extra luxury rollaway daybed available at $45/night including full gourmet breakfast.
  • Plunge Pool Safety: Pool safety fencing & baby gates available on complimentary request.

Resort Etiquette & Smoking

  • 100% Non-Smoking Indoors: Smoking is strictly prohibited inside villas and suites; permitted in open-air garden verandas.
  • Sanctuary Quiet Hours: From 22:00 to 07:00 to preserve uninterrupted coastal slumber and wildlife serenity.
  • Drone & Privacy Regulations: To protect guest seclusion around plunge pools, recreational drone flights require concierge clearance.

Payments & Currency

  • Accepted Payment Methods: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, UnionPay, and Bank Telegraphic Wire.
  • Currency & Billing: Quoted in USD ($); payable in USD, EUR, GBP, or Sri Lankan Rupees (LKR) at central bank rates.
  • Transparent Rates: No hidden resort fees or amenity surcharges; direct booking pricing is all-inclusive.

Airport & Seaplane Concierge

  • Colombo Airport (CMB): 90-minute private air-conditioned expressway transfer with chilled towels & Wi-Fi.
  • Seaplane Transfers: Direct scenic seaplane charter to Bentota Lagoon / Koggala Lake (30 mins from Colombo).
  • Complimentary Valet: 24-hour private secure on-site parking with EV charging stations for guests.
